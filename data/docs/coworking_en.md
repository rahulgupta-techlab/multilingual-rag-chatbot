# Coworking Space – House Rules

## Access
Members get 24/7 access with their key card. Day-pass guests can use the space from 9:00 to 19:00.

## Meeting rooms
Meeting rooms can be booked in the member portal for up to 4 hours per day. Cancellations less than 2 hours before the booking are charged at 50 percent.

## Guests
Members may bring up to two guests per day. Guests must be registered at the front desk and cannot stay without the member present.

## Internet
The Wi-Fi network is "CoWork-Members". The password changes on the first day of every month and is shown on the screens in the kitchen.
