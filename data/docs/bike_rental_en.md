# City Bike Rental – Terms

## Opening hours
Bike stations are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. The customer hotline is staffed Monday to Friday from 8:00 to 18:00.

## Prices
The first 30 minutes of every ride are free for subscribers. After that, each additional 30 minutes costs 1 euro. Non-subscribers pay 2 euros per 30 minutes from the start of the ride.

## Damage and accidents
If a bike is damaged, end the ride at the nearest station and report the damage in the app within 24 hours. You are not charged for damage you report in time unless it was caused intentionally.

## Account deletion
You can delete your account in the app under Settings → Privacy. Ride data is kept for 6 months for billing disputes and then deleted.
